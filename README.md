# Hi, I'm William Campillay-Llanos 👋

### Applied Mathematician | Mathematical Modelling | Agricultural & Biological Systems | AI & Computer Vision

I am a researcher specializing in **applied mathematical modelling**, with a strong interest in connecting mathematics, biology, agriculture, and computational sciences.

My work focuses on developing mathematical and computational tools to better understand complex biological and agricultural systems, particularly under changing climatic conditions.

## 🔬 Research interests

* Mathematical modelling of biological and agricultural systems
* Crop phenology and agrometeorology
* Plant disease epidemiology
* Ordinary and partial differential equations
* Reaction–diffusion and nonlocal models
* Artificial intelligence and computer vision
* Climate variability and agricultural risk
* Proportional arithmetic and generalized Fourier analysis

## 🌱 Current research

* Climate–phenology models for fruit crops
* Spatial models for plant disease dispersal
* Digital monitoring of agricultural systems
* Plant–pollinator–herbivore interactions
* AI-based tools for precision agriculture
* Mathematical frameworks for oscillatory phenomena
